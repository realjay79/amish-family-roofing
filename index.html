Are you experiencing these issues?

Small roof problems rarely stay small. Catching them early is the cheapest way to protect your home.

Leaks & water damage

Stains on ceilings, damp attic insulation, or drips after a storm mean water is getting in, and it can lead to mold and rot if ignored.

Missing or damaged shingles

Wind, hail and age crack, curl or tear shingles loose, leaving the layers underneath exposed to sun and rain.

Rising energy bills

Poor ventilation and an aging roof make your AC work harder. The right roofing and attic airflow can noticeably cut cooling costs.

If you nodded to any of these, you're not alone. Many homeowners in your neighborhood are dealing with the same issues, and you don't have to figure it out by yourself. We'll tell you plainly what's wrong and what it will take to fix it.

Homeowner's guide

Know when your roof needs attention

A few minutes of checking from the ground can save you thousands. Here's what to look for.

Warning signs to watch for

  • Water stains or bubbling paint on ceilings and upper walls
  • Shingles that are cracked, curled, buckled or missing
  • Granules (gritty, sand-like bits) collecting in gutters or at downspouts
  • Sagging areas or a roofline that doesn't look straight
  • Daylight visible through attic boards
  • Dented gutters, vents or AC units after a hailstorm
  • A roof that's 15–20+ years old and has never been inspected

Safety first: please check from the ground or with binoculars. Leave climbing on the roof to the pros.

After a storm: what to do

  • Take photos of any visible damage (yard, gutters, siding, windows) with dates
  • Cover active leaks inside with buckets or tarps to limit water damage
  • Don't rush to sign with door-to-door "storm chasers" you don't know
  • Schedule a professional inspection to document roof damage properly
  • Check your policy and claim deadlines, since many insurers set time limits
  • Keep receipts for any emergency repairs or temporary fixes

Not sure if it's damage? Hail damage is often invisible from the ground. A free inspection gives you a clear answer.

Frequently asked questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your consultation and roof assessment cost nothing and come with no obligation. We'll explain what we find and give you options; the decision is entirely yours.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

It depends on your roof's age, how widespread the damage is, and the condition of the decking underneath. If problems are isolated, a repair is often the best value. If damage is widespread or the roof is near the end of its life, replacement can save money over time. We'll walk you through both options honestly.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Many residential replacements are completed in one to three days, depending on the size and complexity of the roof, the materials chosen, and the weather. We'll give you a timeline before we start.

Will insurance pay for my new roof?

If your roof was damaged by a covered event like hail or wind, your policy may cover some or all of the cost, minus your deductible. Coverage depends on your specific policy and your insurer's decision. We'll help you document the damage and understand the process.

How often should I have my roof inspected?

A good rule of thumb is once a year, and again after any major hail or wind storm. Regular inspections catch small issues before they become costly repairs.

Do energy-efficient roofs really lower my bills?

In hot climates they can. Reflective ("cool roof") materials and proper attic ventilation reduce how much heat enters your home, so your air conditioning doesn't have to work as hard. Results vary by home, and we'll explain what to realistically expect.

What will my yard look like after the job?

Clean. We protect landscaping where we can, haul away old materials, and sweep the property for nails and debris before we leave.
